## Progression Notes

- **Required Days**: The number indicates the minimum time (in days) a character must spend in the prerequisite rank before being able to progress
- **Skills**: All listed skills must reach the minimum level indicated
- **Alternative Ranks**: Some ranks allow multiple progression paths (indicated by "OR")
- **Combined Ranks**: Some advanced ranks require experience in multiple paths (indicated by "AND")

## Recruitment Ranks

### Recruit
- **Description**: A civilian volunteer or drafted individual undergoing initial training
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 2
  - Melee: 2
- **Prerequisites**: Starting rank
- **Default Rank**: Yes

### Conscript
- **Description**: A civilian forcefully pressed into service with minimal preparation
- **Required Skills**: None specified
- **Prerequisites**: Starting rank
- **Default Rank**: Yes

## Infantry Ranks

### Trooper
- **Description**: Standard infantryman who has completed basic training
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 4
  - Melee: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (5 days) OR Conscript (15 days)

### Guardsman
- **Description**: The standard rank of a trained soldier of the Imperial Guard
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 6
  - Melee: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Trooper (15 days) OR Scout (15 days)

### StormTrooper
- **Description**: Specially-trained Guardsmen used to perform various types of special operations
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 9
  - Melee: 9
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Guardsman (15 days)

### Corporal
- **Description**: Non-Commissioned Officer working with their commanding Sergeants in leading troops into battle and overseeing standard regimental operations
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 7
  - Melee: 5
  - Social: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Guardsman (25 days)

### Sergeant
- **Description**: Non-Commissioned Officer who carry out any tasks assigned to them by their officers and are essential to the running of the regiment
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 8
  - Melee: 6
  - Social: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Corporal (25 days)

### Stormtrooper Sergeant
- **Description**: Non-Commissioned officer leading groups of stormtooper into various types of special operations
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 11
  - Melee: 11
  - Social: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - StormTrooper (25 days)

## Scout / Sniper Ranks

### Scout
- **Description**: Scouts are used for reconnaissance and to lead troops through difficult terrain
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 5
  - Plants: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (3 days) OR Conscript (10 days)

### Marksman
- **Description**: Soldier in training for long range engagement and precision shooting
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 8
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Scout (10 days) OR Guardsman (15 days)

### Sharpshooter
- **Description**: Trained snipers of the Imperial Guard
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 10
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Marksman (15 days)

### Sniper
- **Description**: Expert in long range engagement, assassination and operation in hostile terrain. Often alone or leading an independent small squad
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 12
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Sharpshooter (25 days)

## Engineer Ranks

### Engineer
- **Description**: Combat Engineers are soldiers specialized in tunnelling, counter-mining, and engineering operations
- **Required Skills**:
  - Construction: 5
  - Crafting: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (10 days)

### Engineer Corporal
- **Description**: Non-Commissioned Officer working with their commanding Sergeant in leading of engineering operations
- **Required Skills**:
  - Construction: 8
  - Crafting: 8
  - Social: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Engineer (20 days)

### Engineer Sergeant
- **Description**: Non-Commissioned Officer who carry out any tasks assigned to them by their officers and are essential to the running of the regiment
- **Required Skills**:
  - Construction: 10
  - Crafting: 10
  - Social: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Engineer Corporal (45 days)

## Support Staff Ranks

### Medical Personnel

#### Chirurgeon
- **Description**: Guardsmen trained to give medical treatment to wounded in the field in the heat of combat
- **Required Skills**:
  - Medicine: 6
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (15 days)

#### Medicae Officer
- **Description**: Senior battlefield doctor
- **Required Skills**:
  - Medicine: 9
  - Intellectual: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Chirurgeon (15 days)

#### Master of Medicae
- **Description**: Chief medical authority
- **Required Skills**:
  - Medicine: 12
  - Intellectual: 7
  - Social: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Medicae Officer (25 days)

### Administrative Personnel

#### Scribe
- **Description**: Record keeper and data handler
- **Required Skills**:
  - Intellectual: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (15 days)

#### Logistician
- **Description**: Strategic planner of supply
- **Required Skills**:
  - Social: 4
  - Intellectual: 9
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Scribe (15 days)

#### Ordinate
- **Description**: High administrator
- **Required Skills**:
  - Social: 6
  - Intellectual: 12
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Logistician (25 days)

### Logistics Personnel

#### Quartermaster
- **Description**: Manages equipment and provisions
- **Required Skills**:
  - Social: 5
  - Intellectual: 2
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Recruit (15 days)

#### Supply Officer
- **Description**: Oversees large-scale logistics
- **Required Skills**:
  - Social: 6
  - Intellectual: 5
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Quartermaster (15 days)

#### Master of Logistics

- **Required Skills**:
  - Social: 9
  - Intellectual: 7

## Officer Ranks

### Ensign
- **Description**: Fresh entrant into the officer track. Still untested, undergoing basic leadership and tactical instruction
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 3
  - Social: 3
- **Prerequisites**: Starting rank

### Officer Candidate
- **Description**: Advanced trainee preparing for commission. Trusted with command simulations, drill instruction, or small auxiliary units under supervision
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 5
  - Melee: 2
  - Social: 5
  - Intellectual: 2
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Ensign (10 days)

### 2nd Lieutenant
- **Description**: Junior commissioned officer. First true rank of command, usually in charge of a squad or platoon
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 7
  - Melee: 5
  - Social: 7
  - Intellectual: 4
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Officer Candidate (15 days)

### Lieutenant
- **Description**: The most junior officer on the battlefield commanding Imperial Guard forces
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 9
  - Melee: 7
  - Social: 9
  - Intellectual: 6
- **Prerequisites**:
  - 2nd Lieutenant (40 days)
  - AND one of the following:
    - Stormtrooper Sergeant (20 days)
    - Sergeant (20 days)
    - Sniper (30 days)
    - Engineer Sergeant (20 days)

### Captain
- **Description**: Captains act as combat unit commanders, being more of a 'fighting role' than a leading one
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 10
  - Melee: 9
  - Social: 10
  - Intellectual: 7
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Lieutenant (30 days)

### Colonel
- **Description**: Commanding officer of an Imperial Guard Regiment. They are responsible for the troops and equipment under their command
- **Required Skills**:
  - Shooting: 12
  - Melee: 10
  - Social: 12
  - Intellectual: 8
- **Prerequisites**:
  - Captain (40 days)
